# Physics model and authority

One TypeScript body solver runs at 50 ms fixed steps. Catch-up is capped at 250 ms. Input is normalized; horizontal speed, vertical velocity, forces, acceleration and delta are bounded. Axis sweeps subdivide travel into at most 10 cm steps against authoritative AABBs, supporting floors, ceilings, platform landing, grounding and collision. Invalid/out-of-bounds/fallen bodies recover at the registered safe spawn. Transitions clear velocity/input and finale uses distinct radial spawns.

Library uses ordinary gravity and timed kinematic shelf motion. Observatory gravity 3.2 versus normal 14 produces genuinely higher/longer jumps and controlled air travel across elevated platforms. Sanctuary applies water drag, buoyancy, ascent/descent and a valve-controlled current moving a floating seal. Forge applies bounded damped spring attraction/repulsion to a constrained colliding carriage. Manor combines portrait and candle switch bits to slide collision geometry; the wall stops before overlapping a body. Garden applies periodic bounded wind to player momentum, drives a turbine and moves a platform.

All bodies and mechanisms are server-owned. Client position/force/puzzle-success requests cannot set them. Independent visitor instances preserve fair key copies. Timed shelves defer closing when occupied; moving wall/carriage checks prevent crushing. Step tests cover real input movement to all 30 anchors, all finite configurations, mechanism unlock transitions, low-gravity jumps, swim controls, wind, force movement, line of sight and occupied-wall behavior.

`validatePuzzle` combines finite config validation, bounded mechanism simulation, unlocked navigation reachability and jump-height feasibility. This is supplemented by actual solver/controller traversal tests, rather than relying on a flat graph alone. Decorative customization is deliberately noncolliding and snapped; creators cannot block entrances or invent arbitrary geometry.

Limits: axis-aligned geometry, no mesh/rotated convex collision, angular dynamics, general rigid-body stacking or fluid simulation. Translating supports carry grounded passengers using the authoritative old/new support delta, swept in 5 cm pieces against the new obstacle geometry and world bounds. Airborne or floor-adjacent bodies do not attach. Blocked transport stops safely; no client passenger/transform command exists. Continuous carry and detachment tests pass; WAN/browser jitter still needs qualification. Currents/fields are bounded approximations. No prediction means RTT is perceptible; interpolation smooths snapshots but does not eliminate input latency. All anchor routes passing automated traversal is not proof of equal human difficulty. Fairness tuning, WAN latency and sustained multi-room load remain required.
