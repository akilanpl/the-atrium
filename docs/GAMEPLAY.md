# Controls and gameplay guide

Start with `npm ci` then `npm run dev`; open http://localhost:5173. Choose an avatar and nickname, create a lobby, copy the full invitation to friends or separate browser tabs. Join using that invitation. Two to six guests may start; a seventh is denied. The host can replace invitations, lock joins or kick. A short accidental disconnect has a 20-second recovery grace.

WASD: move. Shift: run. Hold right mouse and drag: orbit. Arrow keys: aim camera. Wheel: zoom. R: recenter. E: act on the reticle target. Space: jump/ascent. C: descend underwater. Escape: settings. Optional navigation aids display position/heading. Typing a clue does not move the character.

During preparation you enter your unique assigned world automatically. Walk within reach of an approved anchor and aim the center reticle at it. Hold E at least 0.6 seconds and release to hide; your selected location is marked only for you. A safe default anchor exists if time expires. Test the mechanism, choose one of two variants, optionally select a decorative prop and snapped slot, preview/confirm/cancel, undo/reset, and toggle up to two decoys. Write a clue, then validate/seal. A sealed world cannot be edited. Props cannot block routes.

Library: use clock and brake to open/freeze the timed bookshelf passage. Observatory: align the orrery, then jump between raised platforms under low gravity. Sanctuary: use Space/C to swim, turn the valve and let the current carry the seal. Forge: switch polarity to pull the carriage toward its left stop. Manor: inspect the portrait and candelabra to shift the corridor. Garden: redirect the vane, allow the turbine to spin up, and account for wind while jumping. The last anchor in each world requires its mechanism; other anchors give simpler routes.

Explore every rival once; the HUD identifies the current owner and universe. Read their clue, aim at objects, interact and watch your inventory. Repeating a collected key does not duplicate it. Area and mechanic hints arrive as round time runs down. When all connected seekers solve a round, a visible three-second countdown advances it; otherwise its timer ends it.

Finale returns everyone to separate Atrium spawns. Rival doors allow missing-key revisits; use Return to Atrium afterward. All N−1 rival keys are required. Approach the center portal and target it with E. First authorized activation wins. After the overtime window, only a unique connected positive-key leader may win by progress; ties draw. Overtime gives explicit anchor hints. Results show simple match achievements; they are not persistent account statistics or a solve-time-based Master Architect award. Replay clears secrets, inventory, input, edits, timers and visitor mechanisms, then reshuffles.
